Justin Valentine

I build machines, and I like to do every part myself: the mechanism, the machined parts, the electronics, the firmware and the control code. This site is where I write up what I’ve built and how it works.

In summer 2026 I worked in Boston at Barrett Technology, one of the world’s leading cable-driven robotics companies, where I engineered a new haptic wrist for their WAM arm, end to end. Before that I spent four years building robots in the Computer Vision and Robotics Group at the University of Alberta. I have a BSc in Mathematics and Computing Science from there, and I finish my MSc in Computing Science in December 2026.
