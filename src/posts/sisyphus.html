2026. Personal project, open source (MIT).

A Mac app for riding an indoor trainer while watching something. It holds a smart trainer at a target wattage (ERG mode) over the Bluetooth Fitness Machine Service that most current trainers support, and shows power, cadence and heart rate in a small glass overlay that floats over full-screen video. A tiny Sisyphus pushes his boulder one step per pedal stroke.

It reads heart rate from any Bluetooth sensor, keeps a ride history with power and heart-rate charts, and exports TCX or uploads to Strava. Swift and CoreBluetooth, with no third-party dependencies.
