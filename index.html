Robotics engineer · Edmonton, Alberta

Justin Valentine

I build robot hardware end to end, from mechanisms and circuit boards to firmware and real-time control, mostly for haptics and teleoperation.

I’m finishing an MSc in Computing Science at the University of Alberta (December 2026). My thesis is a haptic wrist for the Barrett WAM, the last stage of a project my lab has worked on since 2014. I designed it and built the final two units with Barrett Technology’s engineers, and visitors to Barrett’s booth at IROS 2026 in Pittsburgh duelled with it in a haptic lightsaber game.

MSc thesis + Barrett · 2024–2026

Haptic wrist for the Barrett WAM

A 3-DOF cable-driven wrist that turns a WAM arm into a 7-DOF haptic leader for a second WAM. Four prototypes at the University of Alberta, then two final units built with Barrett.

Personal project · 2026–present

Variable-pitch hopping robot

A one-legged hopper with a screw-cam leg that changes gear ratio along its stroke, and three reaction wheels for attitude. The controller works in simulation; the hardware build is next.

  • 10.7 m/s modelled takeoff
  • C++ firmware replays the sim
