Justin Valentine

I love building machines, and I like doing every part of it myself: the mechanism, the machined parts, the electronics, the firmware and the control code. This site is where I write up what I’ve built and how it works.

I worked at Barrett Technology in Boston, one of the world’s leading cable-driven robotics companies, where I engineered a new haptic wrist for their WAM arm, end to end. Before that I spent four years building robots in the Computer Vision and Robotics Group at the University of Alberta, where I did a BSc in Mathematics and Computing Science and am finishing an MSc in Computing Science in December 2026.

You can reach me at justin@justinvalentine.ca.
