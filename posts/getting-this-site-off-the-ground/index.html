Getting this site off the ground

April 25, 2026

I want a place that feels more like a bench notebook than a feed: dated entries, stable links, small pages, and no machinery between writing something down and putting it online.

So the first version is very plain, on purpose. A static page is easy to back up, move, search and print, and it’s mostly untouched by whatever’s fashionable in web tech.

The notes should be the boring part. The projects are the interesting part.

Next up, I’m moving a few old notes over to this format. After a month of actually using it, I’ll see which rough edges matter.
